## The bottleneck ProofGate solves

The hardest problem with useful AI agents is not generating smarter answers. It is deciding when an answer is allowed to become a real-world action.

ProofGate puts a checkpoint between an AI decision and execution. Before anything consequential happens, the system must show its evidence, authority, boundaries, intended action, and expected consequence. The result is simple: **no proof, no execution**.
