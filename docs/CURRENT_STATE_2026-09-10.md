# Supreme Computation — Current State in Plain English

**Observed through September 10, 2026.**

Supreme Computation is being used as the governing layer over a growing software environment. AWS Lambda functions, queues, databases, evidence stores, AI models, websites, business tools and bounded AI workers are treated as parts of one governed execution circuit rather than separate universes.

## One brain, many specialized parts

The live Supreme Mind architecture contains **59 digital faculties**. Think of them as departments inside one organization: technology, operations, law, accounting, outreach, market analysis, education, infrastructure, council review and more. They share one constitutional source and one SCQOS governance plane, but they do not automatically share unrestricted permission.

## Shadow Clones

Shadow Clones are temporary bounded workers used when parallel work actually helps. A clone has a parent, role, scope, lifetime and action boundary. Its work remains evidence or a proposal until it passes back through governance. The target is not “AI never makes a mistake.” The target is **a mistake does not automatically become a real-world consequence.**

## Digital twins

Digital-twin tests freeze one situation and compare nearby versions. This tests whether one changed condition changes the governance decision for the right reason. The September 10 Codex test kept READ_ONLY authority fixed and compared a read command with a file-moving command.

## Evidence and receipts

The live environment uses DynamoDB, versioned S3 evidence and AWS KMS signing so consequential decisions can leave durable proof. A receipt is meant to bind request, state, evidence, authority, decision and consequence—not merely say “success.”

## AI model boundaries

Dedicated GrassRootsAI/Anabelle inference and release validators separate **model output** from **permission to release or act on output**. Intelligence and governance remain distinct layers.

## Business execution

SupremeComputation.org has live infrastructure for site delivery, inbound leads, follow-up cycles, bookings, email routing, public evidence, and governed social publishing. External mutation remains more restricted than internal research and drafting. Money movement, legal commitments, destructive changes and other high-consequence actions remain behind stronger authority boundaries.

## Gekyume

Gekyume is the financial execution product layer built on SCQOS. It uses the governance layer to make financial execution bounded, attributable and provable; it is not itself the governing law.

## Quantum work

The repository and live environment contain Qiskit/IBM-quantum work, quantum receipt paths and AWS quantum experimentation. Quantum tools are additional computation/proof surfaces; the core eight-invariant governance law is not supposed to depend on one quantum vendor or one quantum device.

## Implemented, executed, proven

This project distinguishes among:

- **Implemented** — code or infrastructure exists.
- **Executed** — the path has actually run.
- **Proven** — the run left evidence another person can inspect or reproduce.

That separation keeps the claim boundary explicit.
