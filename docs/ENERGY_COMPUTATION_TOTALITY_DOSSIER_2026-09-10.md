# SCQOS Totality Energy-Computation Dossier — 2026-09-10

## Source-layer objective
Unify the strongest experimentally demonstrated paths toward intelligence/computation near biological power scales with direct environmental-energy capture under one closed SCQOS evidence ledger.

This document does not claim vacuum/zero-point energy has been demonstrated as a net power source. It separates established evidence from open claims.

## Prior-art map

### Biological reference
Peer-reviewed reviews place the human brain at roughly the 20 W scale during continuous cognition.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8364152/
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6092772/

### Thermodynamic information floor
Bérut et al. experimentally verified Landauer's kBT ln 2 bound for irreversible bit erasure. Modern processor energy is therefore not a fundamental physical minimum.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/nature10872
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-025-02930-9

### Reversible / adiabatic logic
AQFP and related reversible/adiabatic work demonstrate zeptojoule-scale switching and extremely low dissipation.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/srep06354
- https://pubs.aip.org/aip/apl/article/124/21/212601/3295120/
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04045

### Neuromorphic computing
Intel Hala Point uses sparse/event-driven operation, local communication and integrated memory/compute; Intel reports >15 TOPS/W on conventional DNN workloads and a 1.15-billion-neuron research system.
- https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-builds-worlds-largest-neuromorphic-system.html

### Memory-compute fusion
IBM NorthPole removes off-chip memory from the inference path; IBM reports ~25x higher FPS/W than a comparable 12-nm GPU on ResNet-50.
- https://research.ibm.com/publications/neural-inference-at-the-frontier-of-energy-space-and-time

### Analog in-memory AI
IBM demonstrated a 35-million-PCM-device analog AI chip reaching up to 12.4 TOPS/W sustained chip performance with useful speech-recognition accuracy.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06337-5

### Photonic AI
A 2025 Nature paper demonstrated a photonic AI processor executing advanced models including ResNet and BERT.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08854-x

### Standard full-system power measurement
MLCommons Power measures full-system AC power/energy at the wall for benchmarked ML workloads.
- https://mlcommons.org/working-groups/benchmarks/power/
- https://mlcommons.org/benchmarks/inference-datacenter/

### Electricity from ambient humidity
UMass Amherst demonstrated the generic Air-gen effect: nanoporous materials can continuously harvest electricity from air humidity.
- https://www.umass.edu/news/article/engineers-umass-amherst-harvest-abundant-clean-energy-thin-air-247
- https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adma.202300748

### Moisture + evaporation
Nature Communications reported ~0.78 V and ~7.5 uA sustained >10 days from ambient humidity/evaporation; later work reports >600 h continuous moisture-enabled output.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-31221-7
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55516-z

### Evapolectrics
2025 ACS Nano work reported direct electricity generation from evaporation-driven thermal gradients, including 4.2 W/m^2 under optimized airflow.
- https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsnano.5c10693

### Human-body harvesting
Thermoelectric, piezoelectric and triboelectric systems demonstrate conversion of body heat and motion into electricity.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41378-023-00583-3
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-38852-4

### RF and mechanical harvesting
Ambient RF can be rectified into DC for low-power electronics; piezo/triboelectric devices convert vibration and motion.
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtsust.2025.101291
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s43246-026-01091-3

### Zero-point / Casimir boundary
Casimir phenomena are experimentally established, but no cyclic net-energy device is established. SCQOS keeps this branch HOLD until a closed energy ledger proves repeatable net exported work with conventional inputs bounded.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06824

### Data-center urgency
IEA projects global data-center electricity use from ~485 TWh in 2025 toward ~950 TWh in 2030. DOE/LBNL reports 176 TWh U.S. data-center use in 2023, potentially 325-580 TWh by 2028.
- https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary
- https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers
- https://seta.lbl.gov/data-center-energy-analysis

## What totality says is already established
1. Continuous biological intelligence exists at the ~20 W order of magnitude.
2. Modern digital compute is far above known thermodynamic information limits.
3. Reversible/adiabatic, neuromorphic, memory-compute fusion, analog in-memory and photonic architectures each remove a different major source of computational waste.
4. Useful electricity can be harvested directly from humidity, evaporation, heat, motion, RF and other environmental gradients.
5. Full-system energy can be measured reproducibly at the wall.

## What remains unproven
1. General-purpose human-level AI continuously operating at <=20 W full-system wall power.
2. Data-center-class computation powered entirely by ambient harvesting.
3. Cyclic net-power extraction from zero-point/vacuum energy.
4. A single existing hardware technology solving every layer alone.

## One complete SCQOS execution architecture
**Verified consequence -> minimum necessary computation -> efficient substrate -> environmental energy intake -> closed joule ledger -> independent reproduction.**

A fixed workload is frozen by hash. SCQOS executes only causally necessary transitions. The same consequence contract is moved across CPU/GPU, sparse/event-driven, neuromorphic, analog/in-memory and reversible/adiabatic substrates. Environmental sources are treated together: light, heat, humidity, evaporation, airflow, vibration and RF. Wall input, stored-energy change, harvested input, compute energy, conversion loss, heat export and useful output are all receipted.

## Claim ladder
- Level 1: same verified consequence at lower joules than baseline.
- Level 2: same verified consequence below 20 W full-system wall power.
- Level 3: <=20 W with a measurable fraction supplied by environmental harvesting.
- Level 4: net-zero wall power over a defined duration with stored-energy changes accounted for.
- Level 5: continuous autonomous operation where harvested environmental energy equals/exceeds full-system demand across the defined boundary.

## Eight-invariant closure
- Time: synchronized workload, meter and receipt timestamps.
- Continuity: identical frozen consequence across substrates.
- Alignment: claims equal measured results; no proxy replaces wall energy.
- Genesis: every joule has an attributable origin or is marked unexplained.
- Boundary: include compute, memory, storage, required networking, conversion and stored energy.
- Reference: calibrated meters, papers, hashes, hardware IDs and software versions.
- Causality: changing a source or optimization produces the predicted effect.
- Consciousness/Accountability: named operator, independent reproducer, immutable receipt and public falsification path.
