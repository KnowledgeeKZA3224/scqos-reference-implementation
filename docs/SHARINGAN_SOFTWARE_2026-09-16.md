## Purpose

Sharingan is designed to take a complete sports-betting decision state, evaluate candidate single bets and multi-leg parlays, apply the eight Supreme Computation invariants across the same state, challenge contradictions, preserve the pregame decision as an immutable receipt, and keep post-event verification separate from the frozen pregame record.

The execution target is `ZERO_UNRESOLVED_ERROR_AT_EXECUTION`. This is an architecture target for the decision process; it is not a claim that future sports outcomes are guaranteed.
